7. Padding is the value between content and border.

8. Margin is the value between 2 borders.

9. Margin collapse in CSS is a behavior where the top and bottom margins of adjacent block-level elements are combined into a single margin, 
   rather than being added together. The resulting single margin is equal to the largest of the individual margins involved.
